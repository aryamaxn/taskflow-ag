Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026 • Application: TaskFlow

Welcome to TaskFlow. Your privacy and the confidentiality of your personal tasks are fundamental to how this application is designed and operated. This Privacy Policy explains what information TaskFlow collects, how that information is used, stored, and protected, and your rights regarding your data.

Summary: TaskFlow collects only the minimal profile data required for Google authentication and the task records you enter. We do not sell your personal data, do not serve advertisements, do not employ third-party behavioral trackers, and isolate your tasks so only you can access them.

1. Information We Collect

TaskFlow collects information in two categories: account identification data provided via Google OAuth 2.0, and task content entered directly by you into the task manager.

A. Google Account Information (Authentication)

TaskFlow uses Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0) to authenticate users securely without creating or storing custom passwords. When you authorize TaskFlow via Google, we request the standard profile and email scopes and collect:

  • Google Account Identifier (googleId): A unique numerical ID provided by Google to distinguish your account.
  • Email Address (email): Your primary Google account email, used to identify your account and associate your private tasks.
  • Display Name (name): Your name as registered with Google, used to personalize greetings in the interface (e.g., "Hi, Your Name 👋") and profile badges. You can also customize your display name at any time in the app.
  • Profile Picture URL (picture): A link to your public Google account avatar image, used exclusively to display your avatar circle in the navigation bar and profile modal.

B. Task and Productivity Data

When you use TaskFlow, we collect and store the task information you directly submit:

  • Task Titles: The text description of the to-do item (up to 250 characters).
  • Categories: The organizational tag assigned to the task (Personal, Work, Learning, or Shopping).
  • Due Dates: The scheduled calendar date for the task (YYYY-MM-DD format).
  • Priority / Importance: Whether the task has been starred as important.
  • Completion Status: Whether the task has been marked completed or active.
  • Timestamps: Date and time when the task was created or last updated.

C. Session and Device Cookies

TaskFlow uses an essential HTTP session cookie (connect.sid) to maintain your authenticated state across page requests. This cookie is:

  • Configured with httpOnly: true to prevent unauthorized script access (XSS mitigation).
  • Configured with secure: true in production environments so it is only transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Configured with a 14-day expiration time and persisted server-side in our database session store.

2. How We Use Your Information

All collected information is used solely to provide and operate the TaskFlow service:

  • Authentication & Access Control: Verifying your identity upon sign-in and maintaining your secure session.
  • Data Isolation & Privacy: Ensuring that every task query is strictly bounded by your user ID (userId: req.user._id). No user can read, modify, or delete another user's tasks.
  • Productivity Metrics & Views: Computing completion metrics (e.g., "Today's Progress"), displaying upcoming tasks on the interactive calendar, and filtering smart lists (My Day, Upcoming, Important).
  • Interface Personalization: Displaying your greeting, profile avatar, and preferred display name.

3. How Your Information Is Stored and Protected

We implement technical safeguards to protect your personal information and task content:

  • Database Security: Data is stored in a managed MongoDB Atlas cloud cluster with automated access control, network isolation, and encryption at rest.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your browser, our application servers, and the database are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Server-Side Authorization: All API endpoints (/api/todos/* and /api/auth/*) require active session authentication and enforce strict ownership verification on every database read, create, update, and delete operation.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or task data to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

TaskFlow shares data only with the essential infrastructure service providers necessary to host and deliver the application:

  • Google LLC (Google Identity Services): Provides OAuth 2.0 authentication. TaskFlow's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • MongoDB, Inc. (MongoDB Atlas): Hosts the managed cloud database storing user account profiles, session tokens, and task records.
  • Vercel, Inc.: Hosts the serverless compute infrastructure running the TaskFlow web application.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data in TaskFlow:

  • Deleting Individual Tasks: You can delete any task at any time by clicking the trash icon next to the task. Deletion is immediate and permanent from the database.
  • Clearing Completed Tasks: Clicking "Clear Completed" permanently removes all completed tasks associated with your account.
  • Session Termination: Clicking "Sign Out" immediately destroys your server-side session and invalidates the session cookie.
  • Complete Account and Data Deletion: If you wish to permanently delete your entire user profile and all associated tasks from MongoDB, account deletion is available on request via a GitHub issue, as time permits.

6. Children's Privacy

TaskFlow is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect service enhancements or legal requirements. When updates occur, the "Effective Date" at the top of this page will be updated. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, your personal data, or account deletion, please open an issue on GitHub at https://github.com/aryamaxn/taskflow-ag/issues.
